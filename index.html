What is Ausbildung?

Germany’s vocational training system — a structured path to a recognised profession.

Ausbildung (apprenticeship training) is a dual-track programme combining practical work with a company and theoretical training at a vocational school. It leads to a recognised German professional qualification.

For Indian AuPairs who are already in Germany, Ausbildung is often the most practical and legal route to staying and building a career. The AuPair year gives you time to learn the language, understand the culture, and prepare your application.

Most Ausbildung programmes last 2–3 years and include a training salary from the company.

About Academy AuPair

Built from experience.
Not from assumptions.

Academy AuPair was not built in a boardroom. It was built by a European host mother who received AuPairs, lived through the system’s failures, and decided to fix one part of it.

Why this exists

The AuPair system fails not from bad intentions — but from absent preparation.

AuPairs from India want to go to Germany. They are motivated, they are willing, and they apply in good faith. Host families in Germany want help with their children. Agencies want to make good matches. None of these parties have bad intentions.

And yet — the failure rate in AuPair placements is high. Rematches happen. Children lose continuity. Families are disappointed. AuPairs return home before their year is finished.

The common factor in most failures is not the person. It is the absent preparation step.

No one told the AuPair what a German kitchen actually looks like. No one explained that children eat at specific times and expect specific foods. No one prepared her for the cultural difference between asking for help and being expected to know. No one told her what her contract means, what is legal, and who to call if something goes wrong.

This is not a mystery. It is a structural problem — and structural problems have structural solutions.

Academy AuPair is that solution. A standardised preparation programme that every first-time Indian AuPair can go through before she arrives — and a certificate that tells every host family that she did.
